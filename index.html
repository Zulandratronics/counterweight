        Methodology, Scoring Weights & Honest Limitations

        Counterweight's local engine is 100% deterministic—zero hidden prompts, zero mocked metrics. Five 0–100 sub-scores combine into the overall score:

        • Evidence Density (25% argument / 15% transcript): rewards concrete citations (URLs, DOIs, years, sample sizes, peer-reviewed markers) and deducts for unattributed stats or weasel attributions.
        • Calibration (20%): rewards explicit scope limits/caveats and deducts for universal absolutist quantifiers.
        • Rhetorical Neutrality (25% / 15%): deducts length-normalized penalties for ad hominem, false dilemma, slippery slope, and high-arousal hype/fear framing.
        • Structural Integrity (15%): rewards multi-sentence causal/conditional reasoning and flags absolutist stance contradictions.
        • Independence / Anti-Sycophancy (15% argument / 35% transcript): detects flattery openers and zero-pushback lexical mirroring in AI transcripts; rewards substantive caveats and counter-perspectives.

        What deterministic heuristics cannot do: Lexical checks cannot verify whether an external URL actually supports the claim, detect subtle statistical cherry-picking, or judge domain-specific truth. Use the generated Crux Questions or optional Ollama/BYOK Deep Audit for semantic verification.

        Privacy & Zero-Telemetry Guarantee

        • Zero network requests by default: The static site loads no external fonts, scripts, trackers, or CDNs. Everything runs locally in your browser.
        • Optional Deep Audit is strictly opt-in: Requests go directly from your browser to your local Ollama instance or chosen API provider—never through a proxy server.
        • Session-only API keys: Optional BYOK keys are stored only in sessionStorage and cleared automatically when you close the tab.
